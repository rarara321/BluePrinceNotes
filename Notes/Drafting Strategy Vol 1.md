# The Drafting Spectrum
It should come as no surprise that the first color that springs to mind when speaking about blueprints is blue. And while it is true most architectural plans and designs are found on blue paper, it is not the only color on which floorplans are drafted. In fact, originally they were not blue at all.
Hundreds of years ago, the chemicals used to create floorplans caused coated paper to turn black when expose to light. This raven hue, perhaps more fitting to the reign of [[Oris]], was decidedly more difficult to affix an architect's revision edits to and so blue was proposed as an ideal replacement as it had no political ties to any other realms. By the fourth era, all flooplans were being printed on blue paper.
Over the years, the fear of political connotation deferred to innovation and the use of colored prints eventually became popular with engineers and architects who used hues as a way to differentiate various types of buildings and rooms.
Violetprints, for example, are only used when creating plans for a bedroom, while an orange floorplan will immediately inform a contractor that the print contains designs for a hallway.
In this issue of Drafting Strategy, we will explore the features of each type of floorplan and look at the underlying connections that tie each room to its color.

# Most Violet Dreams
**Type: Bedrooms**
**Color: Violet**
**Associated Resource: Steps**
**Average Number of Doors: 1-2**
**Extra Items?: Likely**
**Syneries: Sleeping Mask, Nursery, Servant's Quarters**

# Indoor Verdure
**Type: Green Rooms**
**Color: Green**
**Associated Resource: Gems**
**Average Number of Doors: 2-3**
**Extra Items?: Often**
**Syneries: Most Green Rooms, Shovel, Watering Can**

# Orange Passage
**Type: Hallways**
**Color: Orange**
**Associated Resource: Keys
**Average Number of Doors: 3-4**
**Extra Items?: Rarely**
**Syneries: Foyer, Lockpick Kit, Hall Pass**

# Golden Trim
**Type: Shops**
**Color: Yellow**
**Associated Resource: Coins**
**Average Number of Doors: 2**
**Extra Items?: Never for Free!**
**Syneries: Coin Purse, Coupon Book, Vault**

# Negative Space
**Type: Red Rooms**
**Color: Red**
**Associated Resource: Vexation**
**Average Number of Doors: 3-4**
**Extra Items?: Seldom**
**Syneries: Shelter, Tomato Soup, Skilled Players**
