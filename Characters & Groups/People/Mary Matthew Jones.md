# Description
Author and mother of the main character, [[Simon Jones]]. Published children's book author under the name Marion Marigold, which was a name she chose to separate herself from the political associations of the Sinclair Family. She has written three books: [[The Red Prince]], [[Swim Bird]], and [[A New Clue]]

# Her Books

## The Red Prince
- Won the Herald Literary Medal Illustrated Book of the year
- Was inspired due to Simon Jones's "inflexible" love of the color red (this was criticized as a thinly veiled critique of the royalist party and the throne)

## Swim Bird
- Released in 1985
- Won the Herald Literary Medal Illustrated Book of the year in 1985

## A New Clue
- Described as unintentionally accurate to [[Bon Margle|Detective Bon Margle]]

# Disappearance
- Disappeared on Friday, 11/20/1987. 13 months before the release of A New Clue.
- Last seen by a close friend at 3:45 getting into a carriage at the [[Locations/Hundred Bells Tea Room]]. She never arrived home and carriage driver never found.
