# Description
Sitting at the base of [[Locations/Mt. Perma]], a historic site significant to the history of [[Fenn Aries]]. Former Home of [[William Epsen|Lord William Epsen]] and kept as a private residence for the Epsen family. Eventually turned into a historic house and tourst attraction in 1951. Eventually turned into a historic landmark in 1971.
