# Description
Publishing house. Most famous for publishing the works of Marion Marigold. Rejected the idea that Caleb manning or Phillip White were responsible for the [[Trinsdale Bombing]]. Stating they were: "victims of an unfortunate happenstance". This is not the first time the publishing house has defended a rogue employee. Three years ago an associate editor was arrested for criminal conspiracy, but charges were later dropped.
