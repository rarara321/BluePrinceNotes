A sigil is a "statement" about a country ore region.

Sigils are made of 4 different aspects
- The Core`
- Refers to the region or country being referenced\ in the statement

		- ![[Diamond.png]] Nuance
- The Dividing Lines (Rays)
	- Its mostly cut off in the copy of Realm &, but it's referring to transportation methods.
		- 8 Refers to naval
		- 4 in a diamond refers to roads and crriages
		- 4 going left and right and diagnal down left right is horseback
		- 3 is train station
		- 5 in a sun is aviation
		- 2 is camels
		- 5 is turtleback travel
	- Then there is the type of ray, which refers to some sort of climate
		- Wavy refers to HEAT
		- Triple straight refers to RAINY
		- Jagged (lightning) refers to STORMY weather
		- A straight line dotted with filled circles is SNOW
		- Bent, curved lines refer to WINDY
- The Motes
	- Refers to the type of society
		- Asterisks refer to tribal
		- Triple dot refers to Agricultural
		- Double triangle refers to militant
		- Rectangle with cube taken out refers to Metropolitan
		- Triple line refers to academic
		- Square with a dot refers to industrial
		- Circle with 4 slices refers to spiritual
		- Flower refers to Poetic
- The Border
	- Refers to the flag of the realm
